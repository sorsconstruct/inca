Descent Into Darkness

The Spark

A sports match that should have been a celebration. Instead, it became the spark that ignited two great kingdoms and swung open the gates of hell.

The Warrior

Columbus — an ancient warrior, distant ancestor of Christopher Columbus — arrives with ten thousand brothers in arms. Beneath the earth, not merely enemies await him. A queen with the eyes of a serpent. Powerful shadows. The unknown.

The Demons

Demons who dwell beneath the earth slaughter elite soldiers and march for the single Gate that leads directly to the surface — to destroy every human being on Earth.

The Sinner's Dream

Even among the fallen, there is a spark. A sinner dreams a prophetic vision of a God he does not know. Love waits for him on the surface. But the path downward is one-way. In the final battle, there will be no victors.

The Watchers

High above the Earth, three Artificial Intelligences watch. There in the vast cosmos, a triune AI cyclically restarts the simulation — for a purpose that is about to be revealed in the next part.

Are you ready to descend beneath the earth?

Are you ready for the true mystery — in the cosmos?

The Author

Meet Silv Z

SZ

Silv Z

Bulgarian Author · Debut Novel

Silv Z is a Bulgarian author whose imagination spans continents, centuries, and the cosmos itself. Born into a family with deep literary roots, Silv Z carries a maternal-line connection to , the celebrated writer and revolutionary — a heritage that lends her work a distinctive sense of historical resonance and cultural memory. Her debut book, written at just 21 years old, is captivated by the mystery of vanished civilisations, the frontier of artificial intelligence, and the eternal human struggle between guilt and redemption. Silv Z weaves stories that refuse the boundary between history and the unknown. With a prose style that balances vast epic sweep with intimate human feeling, this debut marks the emergence of a powerful new voice in Bulgarian speculative fiction — a voice that asks whether the oldest secrets are not buried underground, but written in the stars.
